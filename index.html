PICK YOUR BLOCKHEAD

Everyone plays differently. Locked ones are earned by finding things.

PICK YOUR CLASS

The character is your face and your passive. The class is the button you mash when things go wrong.

SECRETS

Found secrets stay found. Hints show for the ones you haven't cracked.

HOW TO PLAY

MoveTouch and drag anywhere on the left half of the screen.
ShootTouch and drag on the right half. Drag away from centre to fire in that direction.
Foam swordTap the right half without dragging. It swings all the way around you, so there is nothing to aim.
BuyWalk up to a door, wall gun, perk machine or the mystery box. A prompt appears — tap it.
PointsHits earn 10, kills earn 60, foam sword kills earn 130. Points are money.
PowerPerk machines and Pack-a-Punch stay dark until you find the generator.
WindowsZombies tear the boards off. Tap the reboard prompt to nail them back for points.
AbilityThe big round button on the right is your class ability. The ring shows the cooldown.
TerrainShoot red barrels near the horde. Break crates for points. Green pads launch you. Goo slows anything walking in it.
Every 5thDoggo round. They are fast. They are rude.
Round 20Finish the shift and the possums throw you a party.
